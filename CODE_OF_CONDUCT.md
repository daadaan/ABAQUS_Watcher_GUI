# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

Our community is dedicated to providing a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Abusive or derogatory comments
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainers at [maintainer@example.com]. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant; see https://www.contributor-covenant.org/ for the full text and additional languages.
